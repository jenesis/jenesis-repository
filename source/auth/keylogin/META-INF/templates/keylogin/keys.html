A login key signs a person in to the console as a member of one tenant, at the role it was issued with, through the key sign-in. It is for deployments without single sign-on; once OIDC, SAML or a directory signs people in, revoke these and switch key sign-in off. The same keys are listed, issued and revoked by /api/keylogin and the CLI's keylogin commands.
