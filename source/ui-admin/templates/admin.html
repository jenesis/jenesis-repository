Groups

A group holds rights exactly as a person or a key does, and everyone in it holds them too — which is how "everyone in developers may read this tenant" is said once instead of once per person. A member holds the group's rights from their next request. A group with members and no grants confers nothing, so it is safe to fill one before deciding what it may do.

Read caches on this node

Credentials, settings, ceilings and the tenant list are read through a time-bounded cache on each node, so a request pays for nothing twice; a write on this node is seen here at once, and another node's within the ttl. This node is node; every other node keeps its own caches until their ttl.
