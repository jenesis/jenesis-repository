A credential is a hashed access key; its key is shown once, when it is created, and what it grants is given on its own page afterwards.

Optional: what it is for, such as "CI main" or a person's name.
Optional: a duration such as P30D or an instant; left empty, the default lifetime applies.
Discouraged: a key that never expires cannot be aged out. Prefer a finite lifetime, and rotate it.
